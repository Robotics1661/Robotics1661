## 👋 Hey, we're the Griffitrons!

We're a **student-run FIRST Robotics Competition team** at [The Buckley School](https://www.buckley.org/) in Sherman Oaks, CA. Every year we design, build and program a brand-new robot, and we start in the **summer** to be ready for the **spring** competition. 🌞➡️🌸

> 🏫 **Buckley students only!** Come **visit us after school** in the robotics room, any day. No experience needed.
